Built for operators

Three things the terminal does that a retail app will not.

▤

Consolidated book

Depth from every modelled venue is merged into one ladder, so you price against total available size rather than whichever venue answered first.

⚙

Deterministic replay

Every session is recorded tick by tick and can be replayed against a changed strategy. Same input, same fills, every run.

◉

Encrypted at rest

Desk data is sealed with per-session keys and never written to disk in the clear. Nothing is readable without an authenticated terminal.
